Visualization 1: TTC ridership over the years

LINK to the dataset: https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/ttc-ridership-analysis/

    > What software did you use to create your data visualization?
    Python (with pandas, matplotlib)

    > Who is your intended audience? 
    TTC planners and the general public

    > What information or message are you trying to convey with your visualization? 
    This visualization shows how total TTC ridership has changed over the years, highlighting long-term trends and major shifts in usage, with a peak ridership observed around 2015. 

    > What aspects of design did you consider when making your visualization? How did you apply them? With what elements of your plots? 
    1- using a line plot to indicate continuous change over time.
    2- A log y-axis for simplifer visual information.
    3- White-filled markers to improve readability.
    4- Gridlines were removed to reduce visual clutter.

    > How did you ensure that your data visualizations are reproducible? If the tool you used to make your data visualization is not reproducible, how will this impact your data visualization? 
    The visualization is fully reproducible using Python.

    > How did you ensure that your data visualization is accessible?  
    High contrast and clear labeling. 

    > Who are the individuals and communities who might be impacted by your visualization?  
    This visualization may impact Toronto residents who rely on public transit. It may also inform city planners, policymakers to evaluate infrastructure and transportation investment.

    > How did you choose which features of your chosen dataset to include or exclude from your visualization? 
    Only the year and total system ridership variables were included because they directly support showing long-term trends over time.
    
    > What ‘underwater labour’ contributed to your final data visualization product?
    Including locating the correct data source, reviewing spreadsheet structure, identifying relevant rows and columns, resolving formatting issues.
