Visualization 2: Weekday vs Weekend/Holiday Ridership

LINK to the dataset:
https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/ttc-ridership-analysis/

> What software did you use to create your data visualization?
Python (with pandas and matplotlib) - same as the previous plot. 

> Who is your intended audience?
TTC planners and the riders. 

> What information or message are you trying to convey with your visualization?
This visualization shows how the proportion of TTC ridership occurring on weekdays versus weekends and holidays has changed over time. The majority of the ridership was contributed by the weekdays, only a small, steady portion was contributed by the weekened/holidays. The relative composition did not change over the years. 

> What aspects of design did you consider when making your visualization? How did you apply them? With what elements of your plots?
	1.	A stacked bar chart was used to show proportional composition rather than absolute values.
	2.	I used distinct  colors were used to differentiate categories.
	3.	The chart avoids unnecessary gridlines and visual embellishments to minimize clutter.

> How did you ensure that your data visualizations are reproducible? If the tool you used to make your data visualization is not reproducible, how will this impact your data visualization?
The visualization is reproducible using Python. 

> How did you ensure that your data visualization is accessible?
Clear labeling, high color contrast.

> Who are the individuals and communities who might be impacted by your visualization?
